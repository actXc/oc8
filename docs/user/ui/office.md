# Office

**Route:** `/` · **Sidebar:** Office

## What it is

The **living floor plan** of your organisation in oc8. Each department is a
“room”; agents appear as people/tiles inside those rooms.

## What it is for

- See at a glance which teams exist and who is busy
- Jump into a department board or an agent detail page
- Orient new users after login (“where is everyone?”)

## Where you are in the flow

```text
[Setup done] → ★ Office (home) → Departments / Agents → work happens elsewhere
```

Office is the **map**, not the inbox. Decisions waiting on you are in
[My work](my-work.md).

## What you do here

1. Scan department rooms for load and status.
2. Click a department → [Departments](departments.md) detail (task board).
3. Click an agent → [Agents](agents.md) detail (run, chat, config).

## Where work goes next

| From Office | Goes to |
|-------------|---------|
| Department room | Department overview / board |
| Agent tile | Agent overview / live log |
| Need to approve something | Header **My work** (badge) |
