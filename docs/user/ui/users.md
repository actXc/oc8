# Users

**Route:** `/members` · Detail: `/members/$id` · **Settings** section

## What it is

People in your organisation: invite / manage members, roles, and seats.

## What it is for

- Add colleagues so they can open Office and My work
- Attach roles defined under [Access & roles](access-and-roles.md)
- Manage seat-based access to agents / departments

## Where you are in the flow

```text
Access & roles defined → ★ Users (assign) → members log in → My work / Office
```

## What you do here

1. Open **Users**.
2. Add or open a member.
3. Set role / seat as needed.
4. Ask them to log in and confirm they see the right sidebar items.

## Where work goes next

| Goal | Next |
|------|------|
| Fix “access denied” | [Access & roles](access-and-roles.md) |
| Member should approve | Ensure they get My work items (role + being the right approver) |
| First-time org setup | [Settings → General](settings-general.md) |
